Research Question

What matching strategy remains dependable when images contain viewpoint change, illumination shift, partial occlusion, and repeated patterns? This project focuses on robust correspondence, not just the number of raw matches produced by a detector.

Method

  • Compared detector and descriptor combinations across difficult image pairs.
  • Applied ratio tests and geometric verification to reduce ambiguous correspondences.
  • Analyzed failure cases where visually similar regions produced confident but incorrect matches.
  • Organized results around repeatability, precision, and downstream usefulness for localization tasks.

Challenges & Solutions

1 Ambiguous Local Features
Challenge

Repeated textures and low-contrast regions can produce high-confidence matches that are geometrically wrong.

Solution

Combined descriptor-level filtering with geometric validation and explicit failure-case review.

Future Work

  • Extend the benchmark to video sequences so temporal consistency can be measured.
  • Compare classical features with learned local feature models under the same evaluation protocol.
  • Connect the matching module to a small visual localization demo to show downstream impact.
