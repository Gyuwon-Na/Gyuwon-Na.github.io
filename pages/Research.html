Research Style

I present research work as an engineering story: what problem mattered, what assumptions were tested, which constraints shaped the solution, and how the implementation could be improved with more data, stronger evaluation, or deeper systems optimization.

This structure is intentional for large engineering teams. It makes the work reviewable, reproducible, and easier to connect to real product constraints such as latency budgets, robustness, observability, and maintainability.

Profiling and reducing time-to-result for real-time perception loops.
