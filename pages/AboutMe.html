About Me

I build real-time systems that connect perception, simulation, and interaction.

I am Gyuwon Na, a software engineer focused on robotics, autonomous driving, and computer graphics. My work sits close to the hardware-software boundary: perception pipelines, control logic, rendering systems, and tools that help teams move from prototype to reliable field behavior.

I enjoy problems where correctness has to survive contact with real-time constraints, noisy sensor data, and teammates depending on the system during a demo or competition run.

My strongest portfolio work is built around autonomous mobility and real-time rendering. In robotics projects, I have worked on perception, mission logic, path tracking, and deployment workflows. In graphics projects, I have implemented rendering foundations, procedural geometry, shader effects, shadow mapping, and noise-based visual systems.

For big-tech engineering roles, I want this portfolio to show more than finished demos. It is structured to show how I reason: identifying bottlenecks, choosing practical architecture, validating behavior under constraints, and communicating technical trade-offs clearly.
