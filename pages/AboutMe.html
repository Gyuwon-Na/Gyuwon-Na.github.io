About Me

I enjoy building systems where software, hardware, and real-world constraints meet.

I am Gyuwon Na, a software engineer interested in autonomous systems, computer vision, and real-time graphics. My interests range from robotics platforms and perception systems to rendering pipelines and simulation tools, with a focus on understanding how complex systems work as a whole.

I am naturally drawn to problems that combine theory with implementation, especially when they involve real-time decision making, physical systems, or large software architectures.

Some of my favorite projects include autonomous driving on scaled vehicle platforms and building graphics applications on top of custom OpenGL rendering pipelines. These experiences taught me not only how individual components work, but also how they fit together into reliable systems.

I enjoy learning across disciplines and exploring unfamiliar technologies. I adapt quickly to new environments and enjoy understanding how software, hardware, and real-world systems come together to solve complex problems.
